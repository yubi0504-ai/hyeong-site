Dropmate

Client Hyeong
Services Industrial Design
Year 2024
Designer Park Yubi, Min Dong hyun, Bae Sung won, Kim Bomin

Drop Mate is an insulin delivery solution designed for dogs with diabetes and their caregivers. By combining brushing and injection into a single brush system, the caregiver can first relax the dog through gentle grooming, then naturally administer insulin using a syringe integrated on the opposite side. This process transforms the injection into a moment of bonding and physical contact, providing the dog with a sense of calm and reducing the caregiver’s feelings of guilt.

Drop Mate turns diabetes management into part of a daily care routine, improving adherence and making treatment a more comfortable and positive experience for both dog and owner.
